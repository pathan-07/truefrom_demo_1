8. INTERNAL PRICING & NEGOTIATION
POLICY

Maximum Starter Plan Discount: 2,000

Our pricing policy is designed to keep negotiations consistent and protect the value of our work.
When discussing the Starter Plan with a customer, we may negotiate the price if there is a
reasonable business need. However, the discount must never exceed 2,000 from the
applicable quoted price.

For example, if the original quotation is 15,000, the maximum discount is 2,000, meaning the
lowest permitted price under this rule is 13,000. If the original quotation is 20,000, the lowest
permitted price is 18,000.

We should not immediately offer a discount when a customer asks for a lower price. First, we
should understand the customer's budget and requirements, explain the value of our service,
and consider whether the scope can be simplified.

This specific negotiation rule applies to the Starter Plan. Discounts on the Business and
Professional Plans should not be offered without internal approval. Every final quotation must
clearly state the agreed price and project scope.

9. PROJECT WORKFLOW & DELIVERY
TIMELINE

Step 1: Understand Customer Requirements
Before starting any project, we must understand the customer's business, target audience,
required pages, desired features, design preferences, budget, and expected deadline. This
helps us select the right package and avoid misunderstandings.

Step 2: Prepare the Proposal

After understanding the requirements, we should prepare a proposal that explains the selected
package, features, price, delivery timeline, payment terms, and any exclusions. The customer
should approve the proposal before development begins.

Step 3: Prototype Development

Our target is to prepare an initial website prototype within approximately two days after receiving
the necessary information and materials. The prototype allows the customer to review the
proposed design and provide feedback.

Step 4: Website Development

Once the design direction and requirements are approved, we begin building the complete
website. We must ensure that the website follows the agreed design, works correctly, and
adapts to different screen sizes.

Step 5: Testing & Final Delivery

After development, we review the website, check its functionality, test responsiveness, and
complete the agreed revisions. The target delivery time for the full website is approximately five
to six days, depending on project complexity, customer feedback, and the availability of required
materials.

These timelines are targets rather than unconditional guarantees. If a project requires additional
features or changes, we must communicate any timeline adjustments to the customer.

10. PAYMENT TERMS & FINANCIAL
RULES

Standard Payment Structure: 50% Upfront and 50% on Delivery

Our standard payment policy requires the customer to pay 50% of the agreed project price
upfront before development begins. The remaining 50% is payable upon completion according
to the agreed delivery and handover terms.
For example, if a website costs 20,000, the customer pays 10,000 upfront and the remaining
10,000 at the agreed final payment stage.
All prices, additional charges, and payment expectations must be confirmed in writing. Any work
outside the agreed project scope should be discussed and approved before additional work
begins.

11. DOMAIN & HOSTING POLICY

Trueform Digital will help customers manage domain registration and hosting setup. We will
guide them in selecting suitable services and assist with the configuration required to make their
websites accessible online.
Domain registration and hosting expenses are separate from our website development service
fee. The customer will pay these third-party costs directly, while Trueform Digital provides the
agreed setup assistance.
Whenever possible, the domain and hosting accounts should remain under the customer's
ownership and control. We must explain renewal costs and other recurring expenses clearly
before the customer commits to a service.
