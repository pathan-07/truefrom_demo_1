2. Questions to Ask Every Customer

Business basics

· What is your business or brand name? · What products or services do you offer? · Who are your target customers?

Website requirements

· Do you need a new website or a redesign? · How many pages do you need? · Do you need contact forms, booking,
payments, or a blog? · Do you have a logo, images, and content ready?

Design and budget

· Do you have example websites you like? · What style or colors do you prefer? · Which package or budget range are you
considering?

Timeline and ownership

· When do you want the website completed? · Do you already own a domain? · Will you need ongoing maintenance?

5. How to Explain Pricing

Based on your requirements, I can recommend a suitable website development package.
Our pricing depends on the number of pages, design complexity, functionality, and overall project scope.
For a basic website with one to three pages, our Starter Plan ranges from s13,000 to s20,000 (or $350-$400 for
international clients).
For a business website with four to seven pages, responsive design, and basic SEO, our Business Plan ranges from
s30,000 to s40,000 (or $450-$600 internationally).
For a customized website with advanced UI design, animations, or CMS integration, our Professional Plan ranges from
s40,000 to s70,000 (or $700-$1,000 internationally).
Once I understand your exact requirements, I'll provide a clear quotation explaining the work, price, and delivery timeline.

6. If a Customer Says the Price Is Too High

I understand that budget is important. Our pricing reflects the design, development, responsiveness, and functionality
included in the project.
We can review your requirements and prioritize the most important features to find a suitable solution within your budget,
without adding unnecessary complexity.
Tip: Don't immediately reduce your price. First, see whether the customer can reduce the number of pages or features.

7. Project Process and Delivery

1. DISCOVERY -- Understand the business, audience, requirements, budget, and deadline.
2. QUOTATION AND AGREEMENT -- Confirm scope, package, price, payment terms, and responsibilities.
3. PROTOTYPE -- Aim to share an initial design or prototype within 2 days, once the required materials are available.
4. DEVELOPMENT -- Build the agreed website, make it responsive, and implement the specified features.
5. REVIEW AND DELIVERY -- Customer reviews the site, agreed revisions are completed, and the finished website is
delivered.
6. MAINTENANCE -- Offer ongoing maintenance separately if requested.
Timeline target: a 2-day prototype and approximately 5-6 days for full website delivery, depending on scope, feedback, and
when content/assets are received. Do not promise this timeline for every project.

8. Payment, Domain, and Hosting

Our payment structure is simple and transparent.
We require 50% of the agreed project fee upfront to begin, and the remaining 50% upon completion, according to the
agreed delivery and handover terms.
We assist with domain registration and hosting setup. These costs are separate from our website development service fee,
and the customer pays those costs directly.
Before starting, confirm the project scope, timeline, included revisions, and all applicable costs.

11. Key Things the Founder Should Know

· PROJECT MANAGEMENT: Track deadlines, feedback, payments, and deliverables.

maintenance.

and exclusions.
