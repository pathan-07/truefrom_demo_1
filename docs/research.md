## Process (6 steps)

VERIFIED (customer guide §7; team plan §9 has the same flow in 5 steps):
1. Discovery — business, audience, requirements, budget, deadline
2. Quotation & Agreement — scope, package, price, payment terms, responsibilities
3. Prototype — target: initial design/prototype within ~2 days once materials are available
4. Development — build, responsive, agreed features
5. Review & Delivery — customer review, agreed revisions, delivery
6. Maintenance — optional ongoing service if requested

## Timeline
